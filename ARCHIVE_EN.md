# Project Archive Notice

[中文](https://github.com/Zhaoyi-ya/OppoPodsManager/blob/main/ARCHIVE.md) | [English](https://github.com/Zhaoyi-ya/OppoPodsManager/blob/main/ARCHIVE_EN.md)

---

Hello everyone,

After careful consideration, Dszsu and I have decided to officially archive this earbuds project.

The decision comes down to two practical reasons:

1. **Limited capacity**: Both of us are currently carrying a heavy course load, and there is simply no spare time left for project maintenance or deeper research.
2. **Practical bottleneck**: We lack the hardware needed for compatibility testing. Reverse engineering alone cannot reach a satisfactory result, and we have not found a suitable open-source project to reference.

## What happens next

- **Code archive**: The existing code is archived as it is and stays available for anyone to read.
- As of today the project is no longer developed or maintained, and no new feature changes will be merged.

The code remains open source under its original license. Thanks to everyone who followed the project along the way.
